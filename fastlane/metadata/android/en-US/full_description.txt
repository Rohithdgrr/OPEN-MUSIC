OpenMusic is a free and open source music player focused on sound quality and offline control.

Play full-quality streams through a local on-device relay, save songs to an offline vault with quota controls, follow synced lyrics, and keep playing in the background with the desktop widget.

Host or join LAN Jam rooms to listen in sync with nearby devices: the host controls playback, guests follow automatically, with QR invite links and chat.

No accounts, no Google services, no Firebase, no ads. Your library, vault and settings stay on your device.

Network note: streams resolve against third-party catalog APIs (JioSaavn), so anything not saved to the vault needs a connection. Offline vault playback works without network.

Source code, changelog and signed releases are published on GitHub. Updates ship as tagged releases with attached APKs.
